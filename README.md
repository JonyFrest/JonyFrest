## What I'm Building

<div align="center">

### ⚽ FootureX

**Football + Future + X**

*A football technology ecosystem at the intersection of gaming, AI, Web3 and digital economy.*

</div>

FootureX is a startup building a digital football ecosystem rather than a single game. The concept connects
football simulation, player progression, a digital economy and community mechanics into one product, with a
Telegram Web App as the entry point and a **$FOOTX** ecosystem as the economic layer.

| Area | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| **Core product** | Football simulator / manager with player progression |
| **Economy** | Digital football economy, marketplace, digital assets |
| **Community** | Community mechanics, social and competitive layers |
| **AI** | AI-powered functionality inside the product |
| **Web3** | Web3 infrastructure, TON ecosystem, $FOOTX ecosystem |
| **Platform** | Telegram Web App |

**My role:** Founder & CEO. Product vision, strategy, roadmap, team and business development.

**Status:** the project is in active development. The table above describes the product concept and roadmap,
not a list of features already in production.
